About EarningMoneyOnline.co.uk

Last updated 31 August 2026

EarningMoneyOnline.co.uk is an independent UK website about earning more, spending less and building a career on your own terms. Every guide is written for people in the United Kingdom, using pounds, UK tax rules and services that are actually available here.

Who runs the site

The site is run by Ruhul Amin, a writer and self-taught developer based in Hertfordshire. There is no team behind it and no agency. It is a one-person publication, which means guides appear more slowly than on a large site, but it also means one person is accountable for every figure on the page.

The site launched in 2026 and currently publishes more than 80 guides across six subjects: making money online, side hustles, saving money, work and career, tools and reviews, and affiliate marketing.

Why the site exists

Most UK money content falls into two camps. Either it promises figures nobody actually earns, or it is so cautious it never tells you anything useful. This site aims for the space in between: real numbers, methods that have been tested rather than assumed, and plain English.

That means we will tell you when a side hustle is not worth the hours, when a tool is not worth paying for, and when the honest answer is that a method used to work but no longer does. A guide that talks you out of something is worth as much as one that talks you into it.

How guides are researched and checked

  • We start with primary sources where they exist: HMRC guidance, Ofgem and regulator announcements, official platform fee pages and published company terms. We link to them so you can check the source yourself.
  • Figures are checked against current UK prices and rates at the time of writing, rather than reused from older articles or from other blogs.
  • We say plainly when a figure is a range, an estimate or a best case, instead of presenting it as typical earnings.
  • Every guide carries a visible date. When a rate, fee, threshold or rule changes, we go back and update the guide rather than leaving it to drift out of date.
  • Where a claim depends on something we cannot verify, we say that in the guide instead of rounding it up into a confident number.

Every guide is written by hand

Guides on this site are written by a person, not spun out in bulk. Each one is researched, drafted, fact-checked against its sources and then read again before it goes live. That is the reason the site publishes a handful of guides a week rather than hundreds a month.

How the site pays for itself

Some links on the site are affiliate links. If you sign up or buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We are a participant in the Amazon Associates Programme.

Commission never decides the order of a list or which option we call best. If a free option beats a paid one, we say so, and we have. Full details are on our affiliate disclosure page.

What we are not

We are not regulated financial advisers, accountants or solicitors. Everything on this site is general information to help you make your own decisions. For advice about your own circumstances, speak to a qualified professional, or to a free UK service such as MoneyHelper or Citizens Advice. Where a money decision carries real risk, we say so inside the guide rather than in small print at the bottom.

Corrections

If you spot something wrong or out of date, please tell us. We would rather fix a guide than defend it. Email support@earningmoneyonline.co.uk and we will look at it. Corrections go to the front of the queue.
