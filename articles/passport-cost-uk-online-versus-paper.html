The Same Passport Costs Two Different Prices, and the Only Difference Is How You Fill the Form In

A standard adult British passport costs 102 pounds if you apply online and 115.50 pounds if you apply on a paper form. It is the same document, posted to the same address, produced by the same office. The gap is purely a charge for the paper.

The short version

  • Adult standard 34-page passport: 102 pounds online, 115.50 pounds by paper form. A gap of 13.50 pounds.
  • Child under 16 standard passport: 66.50 pounds online, 80 pounds by paper form. A gap of 13.50 pounds again.
  • 54-page frequent traveller passport: 116 pounds online for an adult, 129.50 on paper. Child 80.50 online, 94 on paper.
  • Free for anyone born on or before 2 September 1929, including free Check and Send and free secure delivery.
  • Getting your supporting documents back by secure delivery usually costs 5 pounds extra. Normal second class return is free.
  • There are no refunds. Not if you cancel, and not if it turns out you were not entitled to a passport.

1. The full fee table

These are the fees published by HM Passport Office on GOV.UK, checked on 15 September 2026.

Passport typeApply onlineApply by paper form
Adult (16 and over), standard 34-page£102£115.50
Adult (16 and over), 54-page frequent traveller£116£129.50
Child (under 16), standard 34-page£66.50£80
Child (under 16), 54-page frequent traveller£80.50£94
Anyone born on or before 2 September 1929FreeFree

The paper premium is 13.50 pounds on every category except the free one. It does not scale with the price of the passport, which tells you it is a processing charge rather than a percentage.

2. What the 13.50 pounds actually buys

Nothing, from your side. The passport is identical. The 54-page frequent traveller version is genuinely different, in that it has twenty more pages for stamps, and costs 14 pounds more than the standard version when applied for online. That is a real product difference. The online versus paper gap is not.

Worth saying plainly: if you have any way of applying online, the paper route costs you 13.50 pounds for no benefit. The one honest reason to use paper is that you or the person you are applying for cannot practically do it online, and that is a real situation for plenty of households.

3. The charges that are not in the headline price

Three things sit outside the fee table and catch people out.

Returning your supporting documents. Your birth certificate and anything else you send in comes back free by normal second class post. If you want it sent by secure delivery, it usually costs an extra 5 pounds, and you have to choose that option on the application. If the documents you are sending would be expensive or slow to replace, that is 5 pounds well spent. If they are not, it is optional.

The Post Office Check and Send service. The Post Office charges its own fee for checking your application before it goes. That fee is set by the Post Office and is on top of the passport fee. It buys you a check, not a faster passport.

Urgent services. If you need a passport urgently, HM Passport Office charges more, on a separate published scale. That is the expensive end and it is worth avoiding by applying early rather than paying to fix a late start.

And the one that is not a charge but behaves like one: there are no refunds. GOV.UK states it directly. You cannot get a refund if you cancel your application, and you cannot get one if it turns out you were not entitled to a passport. So the money goes when you apply, not when the passport arrives.

4. Worked example: the Hargreaves family in Kettering

Two adults and two children under 16, all needing standard 34-page passports at the same time.

Applying online: two adults at 102 pounds is 204 pounds, two children at 66.50 pounds is 133 pounds. Total 337 pounds.

Applying on paper forms: two adults at 115.50 pounds is 231 pounds, two children at 80 pounds is 160 pounds. Total 391 pounds.

The difference is 54 pounds for exactly the same four passports. If all four also choose secure return of documents at 5 pounds each, add 20 pounds to whichever route they take.

One decision the Hargreaves family should make deliberately rather than by default: whether either adult actually needs the 54-page frequent traveller version. Online it is 116 pounds against 102 pounds, so 14 pounds more. If someone genuinely fills a passport with stamps, that is cheap. If they take two holidays a year, it is 14 pounds for pages nobody will ever use.

5. Who pays nothing

If you are a British national born on or before 2 September 1929, you can get or renew a British passport free of charge. The Post Office Check and Send service is also free for you, and so is secure delivery of your documents.

There are two exceptions to that, and GOV.UK is explicit about both. You still pay if you need the passport urgently, and you still pay if you want the 54-page frequent traveller version rather than the standard 34-page one.

If you are applying for a passport from outside the UK, a different fee scale applies entirely through the overseas passports service. Do not use the figures on this page for that. And if you are ordering a birth certificate to support the application, the General Register Office charges its own separate fee for that, with a cheaper and faster route if you already have the index reference number.

The honest verdict

This is one of the few costs on this site where the right answer is completely unambiguous. Apply online. It saves 13.50 pounds per person for no difference in what you receive, and 54 pounds across a family of four.

The only decisions with any real substance are whether you need the 54-page version, which is 14 pounds more online and only worth it if you genuinely run out of pages, and whether your supporting documents are valuable enough to justify the 5 pound secure return. Everything else about a passport fee is fixed, published, and not negotiable.

Frequently asked

Is the passport itself different if I apply on paper? No. The document, the processing office and the delivery are the same. The 13.50 pound difference is a charge for handling a paper application rather than an online one.

Can I get a refund if I change my mind? No. GOV.UK states that you cannot get a refund if you cancel your application or if you are not entitled to a passport. The fee is charged at the point of application.

Does the free passport for people born before September 1929 cover everything? It covers a standard passport, the Post Office Check and Send service and secure delivery of documents. You still pay if you need it urgently or if you want the 54-page frequent traveller version.

What does the extra 5 pounds for returning documents get me? Secure delivery rather than normal second class post. Your documents come back free by second class as standard, so the 5 pounds is only worth paying if losing them in the post would be a serious problem.
