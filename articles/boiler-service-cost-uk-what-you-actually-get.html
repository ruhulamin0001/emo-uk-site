You Booked A Boiler Service. What Turned Up Might Have Been A Safety Check, And They Are Not The Same

The two jobs sound interchangeable, they are priced within a few pounds of each other, and only one of them involves taking the front off the boiler. Knowing which you bought is the difference between a valid warranty and a void one.

Ruhul Amin Published 14 September 2026 · 6 min read · Figures checked September 2026

The short version

  • A gas safety check inspects. Pressure, gas rate, ventilation, the flue, and the safety devices. The engineer generally does not clean the internal components.
  • A service goes further. The case comes off, the internals are inspected and cleaned, worn parts are replaced and the combustion is adjusted. This is what manufacturer warranties usually require.
  • Landlords are legally required to arrange the safety check, not the service. That is why rented properties so often get the cheaper job and homeowners assume it is the same thing.
  • Typical price: 80 to 150 pounds for a service, usually less for a check alone, and both should come with a written report you keep.
  • The payback on boiler cover at about 20 pounds a month is brutal: 240 pounds a year buys you a service worth roughly 100 pounds and insurance against a repair you may never need. Against a 400 pound average repair, cover needs a breakdown every 1.7 years to break even.
  • The single line that decides it is your boiler warranty, which usually requires an annual service by a Gas Safe registered engineer with the record kept. Skip it once and the warranty can go.

1. The difference, in what the engineer physically does

Two engineers, two invoices, two similar prices. One of them took the front off your boiler and one of them did not. That is the whole distinction and it is worth understanding before you book.

A gas safety check is an inspection. The engineer checks the boiler is operating at the correct pressure, that there is adequate ventilation and air supply, that gas is burning correctly, and examines the flue, the chimney and the safety cut off devices. What the engineer will most likely not do is clean the internal components of the appliance. It is a health check rather than a repair.

A boiler service includes all of that and then goes inside. The front cover comes off, the individual parts are inspected, the heat exchanger and burner are cleaned, worn components are replaced where needed, and the combustion is measured and adjusted. It takes longer, it makes more mess, and it is the job that actually keeps a boiler efficient rather than simply confirming it has not killed anybody yet.

Landlords are only legally required to arrange the safety check. Most guidance, including from Gas Safe Register itself, recommends a full annual service for homeowners. The two facts together explain a lot of confusion.

Both must be carried out by an engineer on the Gas Safe Register, and you can check any engineer's registration and the appliance types they are qualified for before letting them near the boiler. That takes about a minute and is the single most useful free thing in this guide.

2. Why your warranty cares

Manufacturer warranties on UK boilers commonly run five to ten years, and almost all of them are conditional. The standard condition is an annual service by a Gas Safe registered engineer, recorded, usually in the benchmark book supplied with the boiler.

The condition says service. It does not say safety check. A homeowner who has dutifully paid for a cheaper inspection every year, and never had the case opened, can discover at year six that the manufacturer does not regard the warranty as maintained. At that point a failed heat exchanger stops being a warranty claim and starts being a new boiler.

So the question to ask when booking is not what does it cost. It is does that include removing the case and cleaning the internals, and will I get a completed benchmark entry. If the answer is vague, book someone else.

4. Worked example: the Doyle household in Shrewsbury

A combi boiler fitted in 2022 with a seven year manufacturer warranty. Two adults, one child, gas central heating and hot water.

Option A, full service every year. 110 pounds a year. Over the remaining four warranty years, 440 pounds, and the warranty stays intact.

Option B, boiler cover at 21 pounds a month. 252 pounds a year, which includes the annual service. Over four years, 1,008 pounds. The extra over Option A is 568 pounds.

What that 568 pounds buys. Repairs the boiler is still under warranty for. The warranty already covers parts and, on most schemes, labour. So for four years the Doyles would be paying 568 pounds to insure against something somebody else is already insuring.

The payback flips in 2029, when the warranty ends. At that point, against a typical 400 pound repair, cover at 252 pounds a year needs a breakdown every 1.7 years to be worth having, and most boilers under fifteen years old do not break that often.

Two honest qualifications. If a 400 pound bill arriving without notice would be a genuine problem for the household, cover is buying certainty rather than value, and that is a legitimate thing to buy. And if the boiler is over about fifteen years old, breakdown frequency rises sharply and the arithmetic moves in cover's favour, though so does the case for replacing it, which we went through in the boiler replacement payback guide.

The other lever worth pulling costs nothing at all. Most UK combi boilers leave the factory set far hotter than a condensing boiler needs, and turning the flow temperature down is free, which we covered in turning your boiler flow temperature down to 60 degrees.

Frequently asked

Is a boiler service a legal requirement for a homeowner? No. There is no legal duty on an owner occupier to service their own boiler. The duty falls on landlords, and it is for the safety check. The pressure on homeowners comes from warranty conditions and from insurance policies, not from statute.

What should I get in writing? A record showing the engineer's Gas Safe registration number, the appliance details, the readings taken, any defects found, and the date. If the boiler is in warranty, the benchmark book should be completed and signed at the same visit. Photograph it, because paper books get lost and manufacturers ask for them years later.

Can I skip a year if the boiler is fine? You can, and nothing will happen immediately. What you lose is the warranty position and the early warning. Most of what a service catches, a corroding heat exchanger, a failing expansion vessel, a slowly drifting gas rate, has no symptoms until it has a very expensive symptom.

Does an annual service actually save energy? A modest amount, because a cleaned and correctly adjusted boiler burns more efficiently than a dirty one. Do not let anyone sell you a service on the energy saving alone, because the saving is real but small. Buy it for the warranty and the early warning.

The honest verdict

Book a full service, not a safety check, and say those words when you book. Ask directly whether the case comes off and whether the benchmark book will be completed.

While the boiler is in warranty, do not buy boiler cover. You are paying twice for the same protection, and on the Doyles' numbers that is 568 pounds over four years.

Once the warranty ends, cover only makes sense if a surprise 400 pound bill would genuinely hurt, or if the boiler is old enough to break down more often than once every eighteen months. Otherwise put the difference aside and pay as you go.
