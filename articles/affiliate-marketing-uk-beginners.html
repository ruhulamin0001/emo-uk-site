Affiliate Marketing in the UK for Beginners: The Honest Version

21 July 2026 · 6 min read · Checked July 2026

Affiliate marketing is sold as easy money while you sleep. The truth is slower and more honest: it is real, it can pay well, and it takes far longer to start working than anyone admits. Here is the beginner version for the UK, without the hype.

What it actually is

You recommend a product with a special link. If someone buys through it, the company pays you a small commission. You do not hold stock, handle payment, or deal with customers. Your job is to send the right people to the right product with genuine advice.

What it really pays

Commissions vary hugely. Physical goods often pay a few percent, while software, hosting and finance can pay far more per sale, sometimes a fixed amount that is worth real money. The catch is you earn nothing until people trust you enough to click and buy, and that trust takes months to build.

Early on you make pennies. The people who stick with it, keep publishing, and earn reader trust are the ones who eventually see a steady income. Most quit in the quiet first few months, just before it starts to work.

How beginners actually start

Pick one narrow topic you know or can learn well. Write genuinely useful content that helps people decide, then recommend the products that honestly fit. A single well written guide that ranks in search can earn for years.

Join affiliate programmes that suit your topic. Some are open to small sites straight away, others want traffic first. Amazon's UK programme is the common starting point because almost everything is on it, though the commission is small.

The UK rules you must follow

This part is not optional. UK law and the advertising rules require you to clearly tell readers when a link is an affiliate link. A visible disclosure, like the one on our affiliate disclosure page, is required, not a nicety. Hiding it can get you in trouble and destroys trust anyway.

Affiliate income is taxable. Once your total earnings pass £1,000 in a tax year you register for Self Assessment, as covered in our side hustle tax guide.

Why most people fail at it

They chase quick money, spam links with no real advice, and give up when the first month earns nothing. Affiliate marketing rewards patience and honesty, and punishes shortcuts. Readers can tell the difference between a real recommendation and a cash grab.

How to give it a fair go

  1. Choose one topic and commit to it for at least six months.
  2. Write to genuinely help the reader decide, not to force a sale.
  3. Recommend only things you would suggest to a friend, and disclose every link.
  4. Keep publishing through the quiet months. That is when most people quit.

The honest bottom line

Affiliate marketing is a real UK income for patient people who build trust and help readers, not a fast money scheme. Start narrow, be honest, disclose your links, and keep going. It pairs naturally with the content routes in our YouTube earnings guide and web hosting guide.
