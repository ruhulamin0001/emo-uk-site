Best Free Software for a UK Small Business: Stop Paying for Tools

21 July 2026 · 6 min read · Checked July 2026

New business owners often sign up to a stack of paid tools before earning a penny. For most small UK businesses, free software does almost everything you need for the first year and beyond. Here is what is genuinely good for free, and the few times paying is worth it.

Office and documents

You do not need to pay for a word processor and spreadsheet. Free office suites handle documents, spreadsheets and slides perfectly well, and open the common file types other people send you. Cloud based free tools also let you work from any device and share easily.

Design and images

Free design tools cover social posts, logos, simple adverts, menus and flyers, with templates that make it quick. For most small businesses there is no need to pay a designer or buy expensive software to look professional, as also noted in our AI tools guide.

Accounting and invoices

Several tools let you send invoices and track basic income and expenses for free. This is enough for a new sole trader. You only need paid accounting software once your numbers get complex or you want automatic bank feeds and tax filing built in.

Email and communication

Free email, video calls and messaging cover almost every small business need. A professional email address on your own domain is a cheap upgrade worth making, but the tools around it can stay free.

Security, which you should not skip

A free password manager protects every account and is one of the most important free tools you can use, as covered in our password manager guide. Built in device security and careful habits handle most threats without paid antivirus for many users.

When paying is actually worth it

  1. Accounting, once tax time by hand becomes painful. The paid tool saves real hours.
  2. Design, only if you post visual content daily and templates save serious time.
  3. A tool that directly makes or saves you money that you can measure. If you cannot measure the gain, stay free.

The mistake to avoid

Collecting subscriptions you barely use. Every monthly fee is a small leak. Review what you pay for every few months and cancel anything you have not opened, the same discipline as our switch and save guide.

The honest bottom line

A new UK small business can run almost entirely on free software for a long time. Start free, prove the business works, and only pay for a tool when it clearly saves you time or money you can measure. Keep the security tools even when everything else is free.
