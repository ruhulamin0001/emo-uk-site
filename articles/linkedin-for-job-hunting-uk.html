Using LinkedIn to Find a Job in the UK: What Actually Works

21 July 2026 · 6 min read · Checked July 2026

Most people treat LinkedIn as an online CV they update once and forget. Used properly it is one of the strongest job hunting tools in the UK, because recruiters actively search it every day. Here is how to make it work for you rather than sit there gathering dust.

How recruiters actually find you

Recruiters search LinkedIn using keywords, the exact words for the job title and skills they are hiring for. If your profile does not contain those words, you simply do not appear. Getting found is less about being impressive and more about using the right plain words for what you do.

Fix these things first

Your headline. Do not leave it as just your job title. Say what you do and the skills you want to be found for, in normal language a recruiter would search.

Your about section. A few short, clear sentences on what you do, what you are good at, and what you are looking for. Written like a person, not a robot.

A real photo and an open to work signal. A clear, friendly photo and telling LinkedIn you are open to work makes recruiters far more likely to contact you.

The keyword trick

Read job adverts for the roles you want and note the skills and terms they repeat. Reflect those honestly across your profile, in your headline, about section and experience. You are speaking the same language recruiters search in, which is exactly how you get found.

Outreach that gets replies

Cold messages work if they are short, specific and human. Do not paste a wall of text. Mention why you are messaging this person, one relevant thing about you, and a small clear ask. Messaging a real hiring manager politely beats firing off a hundred identical applications.

  1. Connect with people who work where you want to work, with a short friendly note.
  2. Follow companies you would like to join so you see their new roles early.
  3. Engage lightly with posts in your field so your name becomes familiar.
  4. Keep messages short, specific, and easy to say yes to.

What not to bother with

Endless self promotion posts if that is not your thing. You do not need to become an influencer. A strong, keyword rich profile and a bit of genuine outreach beats daily posting for most job hunters.

Buying premium straight away. The free version does almost everything you need to start. Only pay if you find a specific feature genuinely helps.

The honest bottom line

LinkedIn works when your profile uses the plain words recruiters search for, your photo and open to work signal are on, and you do a little genuine outreach. It pairs directly with a strong UK CV and a sharp cover letter.
